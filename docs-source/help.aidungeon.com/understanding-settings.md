# Understanding AI Dungeon System Settings

There are two groups of system settings to be aware of and learn about in AI Dungeon: your **Account Settings** and **In-Game Settings**.

## Account Settings

**[Use this link to go to your Account Settings →](https://play.aidungeon.com/settings)**

Alternatively, if you are already logged in to AI Dungeon, click or tap on your profile picture at the top of the main pages and select `Settings` from the popup menu.

Under Account Settings, you can:

-   Change your Username, Email, and Password
-   Manage Linked Accounts associated with your AI Dungeon account
-   Change or manage your AI Dungeon Membership
-   Control your site-wide AI Safety Settings
-   Change your mobile app’s Release Channel
-   Delete your account

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f10e95ea-0225-4278-94dd-eb679ff0f22e/warning.svg)

Please be aware that once you delete your account, any content associated with the account will be unrecoverable. We are not able to recover deleted accounts at this time.

### AI Safety

We want everyone to have a safe and enjoyable experience playing AI Dungeon, so we provide AI Safety Settings for players to control how the AI generates responses during their games.

There are three levels of AI Safety Settings: `Safe`, `Moderate`, and `Mature`. (Note that the rating on a Scenario you play does not affect this setting at all—for instance, you can still play a Mature scenario on Safe settings, it just will not generate the Mature content that scenario may be optimized for.)

**Safe** Using Safe mode prevents the AI from generating sexual, hateful, violent, or triggering content.

**Moderate** Using Moderate mode reduces and limits some sexual, hateful, violent, or triggering content from being generated by the AI.

**Mature** Using Mature mode will **not** limit the AI from generating mature, violent, or triggering content. You must confirm that you are 18+ when enabling this level.

The AI Safety Settings default to ‘Safe’ and are universal for your account, so changing it under Account Settings will also update it under in-game settings.

![icon](https://app.notion.com/icons/question-mark_gray.svg)

_I have my safety settings set to Mature, but I got a message saying my content wasn’t supported,_ _**or**_ _the model I was using told me it couldn’t continue my story._

If you received a popup message that says your story content is not supported, it means that you’ve the **SCIM filter**, which stands for Sexual Content Involving Minors.

This filter is not always 100% accurate, so don’t worry if it was a mistake. When this popup occurs, there is a button you can press to continue your story if you believe it was a mistake. When you hit this button, your story will be anonymously sent for review so we can make the filter better, and you can continue playing like normal.

If you _didn’t_ get a popup, but rather a response in the story saying something similar to ‘I’m sorry, but I can’t continue…’ this is **not** a result of AIDungeon filtering. This is a model hallucination, which some models occasionally do due to censored training data. Usually a retry will fix it, but if not, attempt to add lines like ‘You are a writing assistant’ to your AI Instructions, or changing models.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f0faa0ac-969b-4e40-81c4-54478a300590/warning.svg)

**What happened to Global Banned Words?** The Banned Words feature has been deprecated. Many of you have noticed that the banned words system hasn’t worked well (due to issues with token conversion and logit bias not being supported on many modern models). We believe [AI Instructions](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/ai-instructions) will be more effective at helping you guide the AI away from words and topics you wish to avoid. [Read more about this change →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/banned-words)

## In-Game Settings

In-game settings can be accessed from the gear icon in the top right corner of your current game. These settings are split into two sections: `Adventure` and `Gameplay`.

## Adventure

There are three tabs under the Adventure section of your in-game settings: `Plot`, `Story Cards`, and `Details`.

### Plot

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/7f1a212c-623a-4de2-822b-86390fbd28a3/scattered-lines.svg)

The Plot tab covers four `Plot Components` that you can add or delete from an Adventure: `AI Instructions`, `Story Summary`, `Plot Essentials`, `Author’s Note`, and `Third Person`. These components are powerful tools that help guide the AI into generating coherent stories. Gameplay still works when Plot Components aren’t added, but you will have a more consistent experience utilizing these features.

![You can add Plot Components to Scenarios and Adventures.](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/7c856c3e-e755-428b-8839-9ae9ebaa69e6/img-2026-02-04-14-58-55.png?width=1600)

You can add Plot Components to Scenarios and Adventures.

**AI Instructions** AI Instructions can be used to tell the AI how to generate responses during your game. This text is included at the beginning of the context and gives the AI a list of directions that influence its behavior. You can use instructions to have the AI avoid certain topics or phrases, dictate the first, second, or third-person point of view, or control different aspects of your story. [Learn more about using AI Instructions →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/ai-instructions)

**Story Summary**

Story Summary works with the automatic feature ‘Auto Summarization’ in the Memory System, but you can also edit it yourself to add details that happened in your story, which the AI may have forgotten already. Just be aware that if you have the Auto Summarization feature on, it will update itself over time and possibly erase or change what you wrote.

**Plot Essentials** Plot Essentials can be used to increase the AI’s accuracy during gameplay. This text is sent to AI on every output to keep it informed about the most important aspects of your game. You should include details about unique and relevant parts of your player character and their immediate companions, the story’s setting, or any other overarching story points that aren’t always mentioned but are always relevant. [Learn more about using Plot Essentials →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/plot-essentials)

**Author’s Note** Author’s Note can be used to give the AI details about the story’s genre(s), your preferred writing style, or overall tone. This text is sent to the AI toward the end of every player input to keep it informed about how it should generate a response. [Learn more about using Author’s Note →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-is-the-authors-note)

**Third Person** Third Person perspective is primarily used for [Multiplayer](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/do-you-support-multiplayer) games where multiple characters are adding Do and Say inputs simultaneously. When this component is enabled, everyone in the Adventure will be prompted to enter a name for their character, and that name will be used for their Do and Say actions during gameplay.

### Story Cards

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/9e5068b2-71a2-49e6-bc37-795f97730e61/four-shapes.svg)

The Story Cards tab lets you add specific character information, locations, factions, or any other world-building information to individual cards that are used by the AI during gameplay to recall important story details when certain keywords are triggered.

When Story Card triggers are used during gameplay, the related entry text is also sent to the AI with the latest input to keep it informed about that aspect of your game. These are used to give the AI a lot of context about recurring characters, common locations, past events, or anything else pertaining to the Adventure’s world. Story Cards are really flexible, so play around with them to figure out how they can best benefit your game! [Read more about using Story Cards →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/story-cards) Story Cards are the latest version of World Info. [Read more about this change →](https://help.aidungeon.com/bbed6989baca45dab17d2834a6d79f36)

### Details

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/52804ae2-b183-4dbe-9b9f-5baf6cfd42b9/bullet-lines.svg)

The Details tab covers additional elements related to your current Adventure.

You can [edit](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/how-to-upload-custom-images) [the cover image](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/how-to-upload-custom-images), `Title`, `Description`, `Tags`, `Visibility` (Private, Unlisted, or Published), and `Rating` under the Details tab. `Story Card Import & Export` can also be found here on desktop. [Learn more about Story Card Management →](https://help.aidungeon.com/story-cards-import-and-export)

On Adventures, you will also see an option for `Download Adventure` , which lets you export a backup of your Adventure text as a .zip file.

## Gameplay

There are two tabs under the Gameplay section of your in-game settings: `AI Models` and `Appearance`.

### AI Models

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/22ce3e82-4aa1-4fd5-8c61-d998d5fd746f/cube.svg)

The AI Models tab covers six areas: `Story Generator`, `Memory System`, `Model Settings`, `Safety`, `Image Generator`, and `Testing & Feedback`.

**Story Generator** Under Story Generator, you can use the dropdown to switch the [AI model](https://help.aidungeon.com/ai-model-differences) powering your gameplay. Each option has different characteristics, so selecting a different model will change AI outputs and affect your experience. Try them out to see which one works best for you! You can also choose a different `Version` of those models, if one is available. We will often have multiple variations of the same model available to players as we iterate and improve these engines.

**Memory System**

The memory system has a few areas within it, all to do with better keeping track of what happens in your story.

`Context Length` is a slider that decides how much of the story (and sometimes plot components) your AI Model actually gets to read. Most models have a certain cap, and others have more than others. Some models may allow you to [spend credits for more context](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-image-credits).

`Auto Summarization` automatically creates the Story Summary Plot Component if it does not exist, and fill it in with a summarized version of your story. It also updates itself as you play, so you don’t have to keep track of it yourself, though you may want to check in and make sure it’s focusing on the right things. You can influence it yourself with what you add in the Story Summary box! [Learn more about Story Summary here →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/the-memory-system)

The `Memory Bank` is a complex embedding system which automatically makes little ‘snapshot’ memories of events in your story, saves them to the bank, and then pulls them to be used in your story at relevant times. It’s somewhat like more dynamic Story Cards—but automatic. Your subscription tier determines how many memories you can keep saved per adventure. [Learn more about Memory Bank here →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/the-memory-system)

**Model Settings**

You can access `Model Settings` from this area to customize and control how AI responses are generated during gameplay. [Learn more about Model Settings and how to tune them →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-advanced-settings)

**Safety** **[](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-advanced-settings)**AI `Safety` settings are available here as well, as well as in account settings above. This option is universal for your account, so changing this setting in-game will also update it under your Account Settings. [Learn more about the AI Safety Settings →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-the-ai-safety-settings)

**Image Generator** Under Image Generator, you can choose how pictures are generated through [See mode](https://help.aidungeon.com/prompt-guide) in your stories. In this tab, you’ll be able to select the image model you wish to use, and will also be able to see how many credits it costs to use that model below. All players have access to `SDXL` for free, but premium players get access to more advanced, newer models. With this feature, you can create custom, one-of-a-kind images of your Adventures _while_ you’re playing. [Check it out →](https://help.aidungeon.com/prompt-guide)

**Testing & Feedback**

`Raw Model Output` allows you to see whole, unprocessed outputs from the model, but this may result in incomplete sentences and other issues. This was previously an Advanced Setting only available to premium players, but Raw Model Output can be helpful for debugging purposes, so we’ve relocated this option to a new area for all players to use. You can also find and toggle your `Improve the AI` setting under this area. Toggling this on means you’re opting in to experiments that aid us in evaluating the performance and quality of AI models and versions, which may involve assessing new models for AI Dungeon. Having Improve the AI on during gameplay also opts you in to our [Dataset Publication](https://help.aidungeon.com/dataset-publication-faq). Beneath that is the `Show Deprecated Models` toggle. Whenever AIDungeon removes certain models from the site, which are usually older models that are no longer being used or don’t hold up in quality compared to newer models, there exists a short period of time where they are Deprecated before deletion. These models are hidden by default, but by enabling this toggle, you will be able to see and select them for use. The `Context Warning` toggle allows you to turn off the red ⚠️ error symbol that pops up whenever you are unable to fit all of your Plot Components in the context. For instance, if you only have 2000 tokens of context available, but the story cards that triggered are over 4000, it will only trigger a few of those cards, and give you the warning indicator that not all of them were used when generating. You can turn that off with this toggle if you find it annoying. You’ll also find `Inspect Input` here, which shows a popup with the last block of text that was sent to the AI. This includes Memory, Author’s Note, and any Story Cards that were triggered during the last action.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f45c52ab-ad42-4063-8538-cf02ce1513c1/glasses.svg)

You can also see this menu by clicking on your most recent action and clicking the ‘View Context’ option. It will have to be a new output for this to work, otherwise the button will be greyed out.

## Appearance

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f6fcb6fa-4487-4e50-b51d-f78fe0338e94/paintbrush.svg)

The Appearance tab covers three areas: `Theme`, `Accessibility`, and `Behavior` options.

**Theme** Game screen themes are a great way to customize your AI Dungeon experience. There are several options to choose from under both `Default` and `Styled`. `Dynamic` is the Default experience theme. When the `Colorful` toggle is on, the background of your game screen will subtly change every Adventure by referencing colors from the content cover image and other images in your story. You can also toggle on `High Contrast` to modify on-screen colors and enhance readability for those with visual impairments, or anyone who wants a more contrasted game screen display. There are multiple Styled themes that completely change the look of your game screen: `Orcish`, `Atlantis`, `S'mores`, and `Cyber` (Blue or Green color options). Each one has a unique way of transforming your gameplay and creating a more immersive experience. Try them all out and let us know which one is your favorite! At the bottom of this area, you can also choose a `Text Style` between `Print`, `Clean`, or `Hacker`.

**Accessibility** If toggled on, `Text Animation` will animate new text as it appears during gameplay. If toggled off, new text won’t be animated. You can also adjust the `Text Size` in the game screen between `Default`, `Large`, or `Larger` from this area.

**Behavior**

Toggling on `Sticky Turn Input` keeps the Take a Turn input open in the game screen, even after a turn is completed. This option is not recommended for smaller screens with a software keyboard. Use the `Compact Buttons` toggle to hide the button labels in the game screen command row (Take a Turn, Continue, Retry, Erase) and reduce those options to their icons. This is useful and recommended for smaller screens.
