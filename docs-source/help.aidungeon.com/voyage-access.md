# How do I get access to Voyage?

![icon](https://app.notion.com/icons/link_gray.svg)

Voyage is now open to everyone to play at [voyage.io](http://voyage.io)! Head over there now to start your adventure!

Originally, Voyage access was limited to a select few Pioneers, whom we selected by hand from the community to help us develop and shape the platform into what it is today. Once Voyage was more developed, we moved to a waitlist system—where anyone could sign up through the site, and we would let waves of new players in every now and then, meaning it was still difficult to get access if you wanted it. Luckily, that isn’t the case anymore! **Currently, Voyage is in Open Beta**. All players can now access the site, make an account, and start playing a world. It’s completely free to try, with a short demo pool of usage available to everyone who tries it out.

## My free demo is over, but still want to play!

Even if you don’t have a subscription, you can still join and play in any Multiplayer game without using any usage. Our [Discord server](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/is-there-an-ai-dungeon-discord-server) has a place specifically for Voyage multiplayer, which you can use to find open games.

Alternatively, we have tons of public servers available where you can play with other active players, completely for free.

If that’s not up your alley, we occasionally give usage resets out to everyone for big releases, so stay active in the community and watch for those events!
