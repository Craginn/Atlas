# AI Dungeon Discord Bot Privacy Notice

_Last Revised on 4 September 2026_

This notice describes how Latitude, Inc. ("Company", "we", "us") collects and uses information through the AI Dungeon bot, which we operate on our Discord server. It supplements our Privacy Policy, which continues to apply. Your use of Discord is governed by Discord's own privacy policy.

## Updating This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. If we make changes that are material, we will use reasonable efforts to attempt to provide notice to you. Any updated version takes effect when posted.

## **The AI Dungeon Bot's Collection and Use of Information**

The AI Dungeon bot does not collect or store the messages you post on our Discord server. It reads message content over an encrypted connection, only at the moment it is posted, to publish announcements and release notes written by our staff and to bring content to the attention of our moderators where it may need review.

Nothing from your messages is stored, shared with third parties, sold, or used to train AI models.

## How to Contact Us

To ask about information held by our other Discord bots, email [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) with your Discord username.
