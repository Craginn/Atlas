# **Does the team behind AI Dungeon read my unpublished content?**

> No. The team does not read or otherwise modify your unpublished content.

Depending on their input while playing an unpublished scenario, some users may have experiences triggering a built-in filter designed to prevent the AI from generating content. This filter is completely operated by the AI and does not leak nor share the content that it is prohibiting players from generating.

We only read player inputs when given permission by the players for support or when they submit feedback through “[Improve the AI](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-is-improve-the-ai-mode)” and include their current prompt.
