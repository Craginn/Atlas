# **How do I verify my email?**

After registering your account, you should receive an automated AI Dungeon email verification message in your inbox.

If you don’t see a verification email, first check your spam folder (or other auto-sorted folders in your inbox), then check [your AI Dungeon Account Settings](https://play.aidungeon.com/settings) and make sure you don’t have a typo in your email.

If you're still having trouble getting verified, please contact us at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com).
