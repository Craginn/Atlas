# How do I contact the AI Dungeon team?

If you’ve searched for an answer here and still aren’t finding what you need, check our [bugs-feature-request channel on Discord](https://discord.gg/RcYntV44fF) to see if anyone else has asked the question or is experiencing the issue. You can also add a bug report or feature request to that channel as well. Our Discord Staff and Teams are very helpful and responsive!

If you need help after that, please send us an email at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible. Thank you!
