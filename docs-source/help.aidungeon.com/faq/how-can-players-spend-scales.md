# How can players spend Scales?

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/01b5a67f-3f1f-43a1-bc8a-07a31a747579/icon_scales_smaller.png?width=1600)

Scales are one of AI Dungeon’s in-game resources.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/0bb542ae-2279-4fbe-906a-580607d4dee0/gift.svg)

You can earn Scales by collecting your daily rewards or getting tipped by other users. Details about how you can spend Scales are below:

## Speed Boost Free AI Models

Free players can use Scales to purchase Speed Boosts, which gives our Free models up to 2x faster speeds. Speed Boosts are available in 1 hour or 24 hour time frames.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/ba3c5306-2ba2-4e7f-b015-3549cd63f028/speed-boosts.avif?width=1600)

Select the ‘Speed Boost’ banner below the Model Selector to pull up Speed Boost options.

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f5c0c9f0-e952-4d2a-ac17-9a44bbfbacbb/speedboostsexample.png?width=1600)

Since Members already have faster AI speeds, speed boosts are unavailable for these players.

### Why only Free models?

The AI expenses to run Premium models make it cost-prohibitive to offer Speed Boosts for them. These models are also already faster than the Free models.

## Tip Creators

Scales can also be used to tip your favorite creators! From their content or profile, select the triple dot menu `···`, then `Tip Scales`. After that, you’ll be able to choose and send Scales.

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/51951667-82ad-4a6b-9c5d-c55867f40b24/tipscalesexample.png?width=1600)

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/694850bb-fc9b-47df-a6ad-a115b5a0032c/icon_scales_smaller.png?width=1600)

We realize players want more ways to spend their Scales, so we may consider additional uses for this resource soon.
