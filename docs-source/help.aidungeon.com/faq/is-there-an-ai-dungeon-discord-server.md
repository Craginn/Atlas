# **Is there an AI Dungeon Discord server?**

Short answer: yes!

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/49a9bd40-0208-445e-a86e-5149e1b2be50/ic_round-discord.svg)

Come hang out with other players and our devs on [the official AI Dungeon Discord](https://aidungeon.com/discord)!

-   Meet people in the community
-   Ask questions or get help from our Staff and Teams
-   Look for other players to join a Multiplayer game
-   Share your creations and get feedback
-   Read tips and tricks for getting the most out of AI Dungeon
-   Join conversations and learn more about Voyage

**Our Discord server has several channels you might be interested in:**

-   **#ai-dungeon-discussion** is the primary place players discuss all things AI Dungeon; ask questions, pitch ideas, or strike up an AI Dungeon-related conversation with a fellow player!
-   **#community-help** is a great channel for asking quick questions about playing AI Dungeon and getting support from other players around the world.
-   **[#bugs-feature-requests](https://discord.gg/RcYntV44fF)** is the number one spot to post about and submit bugs or feature requests related to AI Dungeon. Before posting feedback or a new bug report, please search the forum to see if a similar post already exists.
