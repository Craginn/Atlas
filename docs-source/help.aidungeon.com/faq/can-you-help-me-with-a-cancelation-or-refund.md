# Can you help me with a cancelation or refund?

**[Use this link to change or cancel your membership →](https://play.aidungeon.com/pricing)**

Alternatively, if you are already logged in to AI Dungeon, click or tap on your profile picture in the upper right corner of the screen, then go to Settings → Membership. From there, you can change, manage, or cancel your existing plan.

If you’re dissatisfied with your current subscription period, email [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) and we’ll do our best to make it right. If you forgot to cancel and stopped using AI Dungeon, contact us and we can review your account for a refund back to your last active date.

Note that if you subscribed through Apple, you’ll need to request a refund through Apple, as their policy doesn’t allow us to handle those refunds ourselves.
