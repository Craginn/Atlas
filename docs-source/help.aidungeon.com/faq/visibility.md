# What’s the difference between Published, Unlisted, and Private Visibility?

**Published** content is public and available for anyone online to see, read, or play. These are subject to moderation as per our [Community Guidelines](https://help.aidungeon.com/community-guidelines).

**Unlisted** content is public only to those who have access to the share link of your adventure. It will not show up in discovery or any kind of search.

**Private** content (the default setting) is only available to the player who created it. They are encrypted on our server, and we make sure that even Latitude staff cannot see them without your consent. In some cases, a developer may need access to your adventure in order to fix a corrupted/bugged part of your story, or you might provide metadata about your content when you [Report an Issue](https://help.aidungeon.com/feedback-ids) in-game. In both cases, a Feedback ID is needed from you, which can only be generated when you use the Report an Issue feature.

-   While no human beings will read your Private content unless you give express consent, the AI is still set with a [filter that stops extremist content](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-triggers-the-ai-filter), regardless of whether or not the Adventure is published.

All scenarios are considered to be **Drafts** until they are published, meaning that they are always private, visible only to you, until publication. You can also make drafts of Published scenarios, but the changes in your draft will not be visible to others until you publish again.

Published scripts count as content, and must follow these guidelines as well.

[To learn more about drafts and Scenarios, click here →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-scenarios)

# How do I publish content?

Firstly, always ensure that the content you wish to publish meets our [Community Guidelines](https://help.aidungeon.com/community-guidelines). Private content does not have to follow these, but whenever you make something public, you thereby agree to follow the guidelines for the safety of everyone on the platform.

Before being published, your content must first be run through our [rating checker](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/ai-rating-instructions). An AI tool will read and appropriately rate it on a scale of Everyone, Teen, Mature, and Unrated, depending on how appropriate it is to certain ages.

The dropdown used to publish.

Once your content has been rated, it is free to publish! You can continue to make changes afterwards, but any new changes must be published again and re-checked by the tool.

![icon](https://app.notion.com/icons/robot_lightgray.svg)

Even though an AI will be rating your material no matter what, it’s still important to have a good grasp on the Community Guidelines before you publish. **AI can make mistakes**, but humans will always have the better sense of what is right and what is wrong.

AI Dungeon deeply understands that—which is why, at your request, you can ask for a human to review your content if you think the AI tool has gotten it wrong. Ping `ata523100` on Discord for a Scenario Review.
