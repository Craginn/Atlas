# My Stuff/Your Profile

The content you make in AI Dungeon by playing [Adventures](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-adventures) or creating [Scenarios](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-scenarios) is automatically saved to your account and can be found through your Saves and your Profile, otherwise known as “My Stuff”. You **must** be logged in to ensure your games and creations are saved.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/1d25a625-b2e4-46ec-aafb-b727ba1aa81d/one-person.svg)

You can get to these areas by clicking the folder icon on the top bar for Saves, and your Profile Picture to get to your profile.

## Your Profile

Right underneath your profile, you'll see some tabs: `Scenarios`, `Adventures`, `Scripts`, `Bookmarks`, and `Trash`. Use these to navigate between [Scenarios](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-scenarios) you've created, [Adventures](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-adventures) you've played, [Scripts](https://help.aidungeon.com/what-are-scripts-and-how-do-you-install-them) you've made, content you've bookmarked, or content you've deleted.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/88f76bf2-d379-4527-8210-b99d1fdcc7a6/pencil.svg)

You can also `Edit` your profile picture, username, and bio from here.

## Your Content

Right underneath your profile, you’ll see some tabs: `Scenarios`, `Adventures`, `Bookmarks`, and `Trash`. Use these to navigate between [Scenarios](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-scenarios) you’ve created, [Adventures](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-adventures) you’ve played, content you’ve bookmarked, or content you’ve deleted.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/e2da48dd-1afa-4806-a3c4-e2102e3f62c8/search.svg)

If you have a lot of content, use the search icon or search bar at the top of your screen and type in some keywords to look up a specific Adventure or Scenario. Try to set descriptive titles and use detailed tags early on so you can easily find this content later.

### Scenarios & Adventures

Use the three dots `···` in the upper right corner of Scenario and Adventure content cards to open an easy-access menu of options: `Edit`, `Bookmark`, `Publish`, `Read`, `Duplicate`, and `Delete`. Clicking or tapping into the content also brings up these options in a full screen view.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/f51521c4-5108-4be0-a913-1aa828ab2d88/pencil.svg)

‘Edit’ accesses the Scenario and Adventure editing popups, letting you modify the Prompt, Plot Components, Story Cards, and other Details of that content.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/fca6fb6f-4ff3-4fc6-a27b-539b1e71728c/bookmark.svg)

‘Bookmark’ adds that content to your Bookmarks tab. This is useful for keeping track of your most important Scenarios and Adventures. You can remove Bookmarks through the same popup menu.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/e933178d-bf65-4499-9391-46d2d6e5ae09/book.svg)

‘Publish’ asks you to rate the content, then changes that content’s [visibility](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/visibility) from Private to Published. This makes it available for everyone on AI Dungeon to play or read. Scenarios and Adventures can only be published once you’ve given them a Title and Description. Make sure you’re following our [Community Guidelines](https://help.aidungeon.com/community-guidelines) when publishing content. Once your content is Published, the popup menu will display `Share` in its place.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/93c9359e-d1ad-407b-86f8-84d5751a2c30/share.svg)

Share generates a link you can copy and post outside of AI Dungeon to share your content with other people.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/0890f470-cc44-47ff-acf5-33c2a6b48288/glasses.svg)

Read is an Adventure-only option. It pulls up the entire text of your story in a read-only view so you can easily skim through or pore over your gameplay. You can change the text size in this view through the two `Aa` buttons in the top right corner of this screen, but this will change the text size in the game screen as well.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/95826db8-d205-41b4-8cac-408d2a119773/square-corner-line.svg)

Duplicate makes a copy of that content. This is useful if you want two versions of the same Scenario or Adventure to experiment with and try different gameplay styles or starting setups.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/cb576147-04ff-4726-b980-f2f57bb3a04e/trash.svg)

Delete moves that content to your Trash.

### Scripts

The Scripts tab shows Scripts you've made. Your private Scripts appear here too, but only you can see them—other players only see the Scripts you've published.

Scripts work a little differently from Scenarios: they don't have a Play button, since you add them to an Adventure instead. To use one of the Scripts you’ve made, open any Adventure and go to Game settings → Scripts → **“+ Add Script”**, then look under **My Scripts**.

### Bookmarks

Bookmarks are useful for keeping track of your most important content or saving other players' content for easy access later. Scripts you save from Discover show up here too—choose Scripts as the content type to see them. To unbookmark content, use the same three dots to open the popup menu and select `Remove Bookmark`.

### Trash

The Trash tab contains all of the Scenarios, Adventures, and Scripts you Delete. Going to the trash and clicking or tapping on the same three dots opens up some other options: `Restore`, `Permanently Delete`, and `Empty Trash`.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/51ee3597-d568-4ec2-938d-dfd59c328e1e/loop-back.svg)

Restore takes that content out of the Trash and puts it back under the associated content tab. Use Restore to retrieve content you may have mistakenly deleted or changed your mind about deleting.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/105e8353-7bb2-4633-bc05-1f15368d55bf/trash.svg)

Permanently Delete completely removes that content from your Stuff. You will not be able to access or Restore content once you Permanently Delete it, so always make sure you’re not getting rid of important content when choosing this option.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/9eb12f7f-1bbe-4d8f-a914-dd5627aad638/trash.svg)

Empty Trash is just Permanently Delete but for everything currently in your Trash. Using Empty Trash completely removes **all** content from this tab and you will not be able to get it back, so double make sure you’re not getting rid of important content when choosing this option.

There is currently no limit to the number of Adventures, Scenarios, Scripts, and Bookmarks you can have under My Stuff, so you don’t _need_ to delete content. Never delete anything you’ll want to play, read, or share again!
