# Why do I need an account to play?

We host the AI models on the cloud to enable seamless play on any web browser or mobile device of your choosing.

We also store your adventures in the cloud, so you can always access and play them through your account, even if you switch between devices. You access them through “My Stuff”, which is linked to your account. And, if you choose to publish your stories, they will be listed under your account name.

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/d08ed2ae-2efe-4c6c-95cb-314c4f544a4f/Untitled.png?width=1600)

In short, having an activated account on AI Dungeon lets us give you a full experience and lets you keep your adventures safe.
