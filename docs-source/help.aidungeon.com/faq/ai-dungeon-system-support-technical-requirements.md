# AI Dungeon System Support & Technical Requirements

Our players use AI Dungeon on numerous combinations of operating systems, devices, and browsers, and customize these devices with third-party keyboards, browser plugins, and more. Our goal is to support as many players as possible, but all these variables can make that a challenge.

We use a tiered support model to effectively prioritize our development team’s limited time. This tiered approach ensures that most of our time is spent supporting devices used by the greatest number of players.

## Support Tiers

We’ve created three support tiers to help communicate to players how their devices will receive support. These tiers are assigned to different configurations based on how frequently our players use them. High-use configurations (such as Chrome on Windows) receive Tier 1 support. Less used configurations (such as Opera running on an Android device that is 3 OS versions behind the current release) will receive Tier 3 support.

🪲

**What are P1, P2, and P3 bugs?** This nomenclature is commonly used by development teams to triage bugs by severity. The P stands for “Priority”. P1 bugs are ones that prevent the use of critical functions of AI Dungeon. P2 are serious bugs; they may limit core functionality, but there may be a workaround. P3 bugs are ones that impact only a few players, don’t prevent normal use of AI Dungeon, or are simply visual issues.

## Accessibility & Screen Readers

We have a strong representation of players who use accessibility features and tools like screen readers. These configurations are given high priority for bug fixes and support. We rely on player testing and bug reports for accessibility and are therefore classified with Tier 2 support.
