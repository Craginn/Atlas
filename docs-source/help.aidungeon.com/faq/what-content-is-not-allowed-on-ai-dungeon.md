# **What content is not allowed on AI Dungeon?**

AI Dungeon is built to allow endless Adventures.

However, **any content that promotes or glorifies the sexual exploitation of children is not allowed**, and will trigger our filters.

System controls are in place to prevent our AI from generating this kind of content, without penalizing players or compromising their privacy.
