# What triggers the AI filter?

The only intended trigger for the AI filter is content suggesting, promoting, or glorifying the sexual exploitation of children.

If you encounter the filter, you will be prompted to either report the trigger as a false positive, or not report it and go back to your adventure. If you choose not to report, you can try again or edit your adventure to continue playing.

If you report the trigger as a false positive, a snapshot of your current adventure's context will be included in the report for the team to review and use to help further improve the filter's accuracy, and you will be able to continue your adventure without the filter triggering for 30 minutes.

When a false positive is reported, it will use one of your 5 account bypasses. Once all 5 bypasses for your account are used, they are gone. If you believe the bypasses were all used on genuine false positives, you can contact our support team at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) to have your reports reviewed. If all five are found to be false positives, we will add five additional bypasses back to your account.
