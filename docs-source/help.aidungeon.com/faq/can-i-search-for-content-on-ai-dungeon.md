# Can I search for content on AI Dungeon?

Yes you can! On desktop, use the search bar at the top of [play.aidungeon.com](https://play.aidungeon.com) to type in some keywords of what you’re looking for, then press enter.

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/58a3077b-6156-4d00-845e-7aedc377e58e/search.svg)

On mobile, use the magnifying glass icon at the top of the screen to pull up our search page, then type in some keywords and press return.

Use the `Content Type` option under `Filters` to switch between playable [Scenarios](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-scenarios) from AI Dungeon creators, and [Scripts](https://help.aidungeon.com/what-are-scripts-and-how-do-you-install-them) you can add to your own Adventures. You can also just scroll through our entire `Discover` section and see if anything interesting jumps out!

Use the `Filters` button next to the content type tabs to change how search results appear:

-   Sort content by Best Match; New; Popular; Trending; or Recently Updated
-   Show content from All Time; Past Year; Past Month; Past Week; or Today
-   Show content ratings that are only E, T, M, or Unrated—or any mix between the four
-   Show only Following, Third Person, and/or Multiplayer

By default, these filters are set to Trending, All Time, and E and T, but you can customize and control these options through the popup.
