# What does excessive usage mean?

We offer unlimited AI use for all our models, no matter what tier you’re on.

This means that we have to put certain limits in place to prevent the abuse of AI Dungeon, particularly from suspected botting behavior. If we suspect unusual activity due to excessive usage from an account, the account may have its usage reduced or be subject to account suspensions or bans.

Our system is designed with multiple checks and parameters so that regular players will not be impacted by the limits. If you feel you were inadvertently affected by any, please reach out to our support team at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com), and we can help you.
