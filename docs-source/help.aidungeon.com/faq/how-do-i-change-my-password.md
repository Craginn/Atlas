# How do I change my password?

**[Use this link to change your password →](https://play.aidungeon.com/settings/change-password)**

Alternatively, if you are already logged in to AI Dungeon, click or tap on your profile picture in the upper right corner of the screen, then choose Settings → Change Password.

**[Use this link if you have forgotten your password →](https://play.aidungeon.com/forgot-password)**

Please check your spam folder (or other auto-sorted folders in your inbox) if you don’t receive the email after a couple of minutes.

If you are experiencing problems with third-party authorization (Google or Apple logins), use the same “Forgot Password” link as above to create a new password associated with your AI Dungeon account email address.

If you need any further help, you can reach out to our support email, [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com).
