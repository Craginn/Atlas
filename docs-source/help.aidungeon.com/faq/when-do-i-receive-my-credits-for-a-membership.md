# When do I receive my Credits for a membership?

[Credits](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-image-credits) are granted to members after successful payment of their chosen subscription. **This means that Credits are not available during the trial period at the start of a subscription.**

Members who sign up for the 6-month and 12-month plans will receive the entire allotment of Credits at the beginning of their paid membership, rather than distributed on a monthly schedule.

If you’re having trouble with your plan or need help, please reach out to [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com).
