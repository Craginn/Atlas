# **AI Dungeon: The Basics**

### Choose Your Adventure

[Quick Start](https://play.aidungeon.com/scenario/1r0T3TXT/new-adventure/play) a new game using the Play button and choose a setting, character, and name, then watch as the AI fills in details for your Adventure.

Or, pick from hundreds of community-published, predefined Scenarios to start your Adventure. The [Discover](https://play.aidungeon.com/discover) section is a great place to explore and find content you want to play.

### **Take A Turn**

This is a roleplay experience, so Take a Turn giving your character [Do](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/do-mode) or [Say](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/say-mode) actions. Or, write your own text with [Story](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/story-mode) to move the plot along how you want. It’s your Adventure: anything you can type, you can do!

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/8e65fa5e-075a-4dd4-b5b2-b5745e3d7adf/TAT_1.gif?width=1000)

The AI will generate responses from your inputs and can advance the story to introduce characters, world events, and other elements for you to interact with during gameplay.

Every story is unique and unexpected.

### **Make it Yours**

You are the main character _and_ director of your story.

Use Undo, Redo, Retry, and Edit to control the action. Use [Plot Components](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/plot-components) and [Story Cards](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/story-cards) to keep the AI coherent and consistent. 

Live out your wild ideas however you want: you’re in charge!
