# About Shadow Tiers

## What are Shadow Tiers?

Shadow Tiers were designed, specifically, for players who were spending lots of money buying individual credit packages.

Those players often gave feedback about wanting a more fluid and immersive experience, without worrying about their credits running out and having to buy more. Many players also gave feedback about how the monthly credits offered in the packages wasn’t enough for the entire month.

Thus, we created **Shadow Tiers**.

![icon](https://app.notion.com/icons/darks_gray.svg)

The prices of Shadow Tiers can be uncomfortable.

It’s important to know that Shadow Tiers are _not_ the right option for the average player—not even the above average player.

Shadow Tiers provide unprecedented access to some of our most expensive models and context lengths, ensuring you get the most out of AI Dungeon, without having to buy extra credits throughout the month. However, most users don’t even approach the monthly usage that the average Shadow member has.

## How to Sign Up

If you’d like to sign up for Shadow Tiers, you must do so from the web, as these are not available from the Android or iOS mobile apps. Only monthly plans are available for each Shadow Tier—there are no annual options or discounts.

**If you already have an active subscription through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, you must cancel this subscription first before signing up for a Shadow Tier.**

**[Click here to view the Shadow Membership Page →](https://play.aidungeon.com/shadow-members)**

## What benefits do Shadow Tiers get?

There are three Shadow tiers—Banshee, Reaper, and Apocalypse, in order of least expensive to most expensive.

### Regular Benefits

The most important benefit a Shadow member gets is access to _much_ more context than the regular plans. Each new tier doubles the context of the last tier (for models that can be doubled). If a model is 4k context on Ultimate, then it’s 8k on Banshee, 16k on Reaper, and 32k on Apocalypse. Some models may not be doubled if they are already at their maximum Context.

Additionally, like the normal tiers, Shadow tiers get more Memories, more Credits, and lower cost (sometimes even Free) Image Generation.

For a full, up-to-date overview of all the benefits, check out the [Shadow Membership Page](https://play.aidungeon.com/shadow-members).

### Shadow Clan

One special benefit that Shadow Tiers get access to is the ability to add other members to their ‘Clan’, sort of like a family plan. The Shadow Clan will grant them premium access to AI Dungeon at no additional cost to you or them. Anyone in your Shadow Clan will have premium access for as long as you’re a Shadow Tier member.

Adding a Shadow Clan member takes up a certain number of slots as determined by the plan you wish to give them—you have a number of slots as decided by your Shadow tier. A Journey plan counts for 1 slot, Legend counts for 2 slots, Mythic counts for 3 slots, and Ultimate counts for 6 slots.

If you’re a Shadow Tier member and would like to set up a Shadow Clan, simply [click here to be taken to your Shadow Clan Settings](https://play.aidungeon.com/settings/shadow-clan). Or, while within the app or on the website, navigate to your Settings → Shadow Clan.

After you add someone to your Shadow Clan, they will receive and in-game notification detailing which plan they received and from who.

### Shadow-Exclusive Discord Channel

In addition, you will also get access to a special channel on our [Discord](https://aidungeon.com/discord), where you can talk to other Shadow members (and also any members of your Shadow Clan).

To get access to this Discord channel, [click here to be taken to your Shadow Clan Settings](https://discord.com/channels/@me/1291440075573563393/1470529092209086742), or, while within the app or on the website, navigate to your Settings → Shadow Clan and connect your Discord account there.

Similarly, any of your Shadow Clan members can also get access to this Discord channel by [clicking here to be taken to their linked account settings](https://play.aidungeon.com/settings/linked-accounts), or, while within the app or on the website, navigating to their Settings → Linked Accounts and connecting their Discord account there.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What if my Shadow Clan member already has a subscription?

Their account will utilize the highest access level granted to them, whether through their own subscription or through their Shadow Clan access.

If they have a subscription and would prefer to use your Shadow Clan access, they’ll need to manually cancel their subscription.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

When you cancel, your Shadow Clan will lose premium benefits when your membership expires.

Can I change who’s apart of my Shadow Clan?

Yes—members of your Shadow Clan are configurable at any time.

I’m having trouble setting up my Shadow Clan/getting access to the Discord Channel.

Please reach out to us at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) if you run into issues, and our team will be happy to help.

What happened to the **Wraith** Tier?

The Wraith tier was deprecated on April 22nd, 2026. We deprecated this tier for the new **Ultimate** tier, which is less expensive (at $99 USD vs. $126 USD) and available as an official option on our main subscription page, able to be purchased by mobile users.

We never expected the Wraith tier to gain so much popularity and attraction, especially since it was meant to be hidden. We decided that since it is so popular, we would make it official, and cheaper to buy.

If you’re subscribed to the Wraith Tier, no action is needed from you to change as long as your subscription renews. We’ll continue to support Wraith, but no new subscribers will be allowed to purchase the tier. Wraith afterwards was given a +25% boost to context to make up for Ultimate replacing it.

## **Why can’t I subscribe to Shadow Tiers on the App?**

There are a few reasons why there isn’t native support for App Store purchasing:

1.  By providing App Store support, we would have to misleadingly advertise having in-app purchases up to hundreds of dollars, even though most players would never need to think about subscribing to these tiers.
2.  Purchases on the App Store aren’t always handled by us, which means the amount of support we can give to them is limited. We _never_ want someone to buy these tiers and then struggle to receive support.
3.  Finally, purchasing on the App Store adds on fees and taxes to the original price. These fees get bigger the larger the purchase—and these purchases are _large_. By only using Web payments, we can avoid unnecessarily taxing players.

## How to Reach Support

We realize the process for signing up to these web-only tiers is bumpier than our other tiers. Please reach out to us at [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) if you run into issues accessing a Shadow Tier, and our team will be happy to help you upgrade one-on-one.
