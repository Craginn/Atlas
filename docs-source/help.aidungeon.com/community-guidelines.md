# AI Dungeon Community Guidelines

The purpose of these guidelines is to build a safe, fun space in which we can all find, create, and enjoy AI experiences within Latitude products. Every creator is expected to set appropriate content ratings for their published content based on the content ratings below. It's highly encouraged to take the suggested content rating of the AI Rating Tool found at the bottom of the details tab of your Scenario into consideration when doing so.

We reserve the right to update these Community Guidelines at any time, with or without notice, in line with our efforts to protect the integrity of our community experience. Any disputes or gray areas will be handled at the moderators' discretion to align with the safety expectations of the broader player community, and any examples used in these guidelines are for illustrative purposes only and not intended as an exhaustive list of what is or is not allowed. For questions regarding, or disputing of a moderation decision, clarification about a specific guideline, or how to best abide by these guidelines, email [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) — Thank you!

These guidelines apply to everything publicly posted on the platform, this includes: Scenarios, Adventures, Cover images, Comments, Usernames and Bios. Usernames and Bios must never exceed a Teen rating nor include any mention of higher ratings, while Comments mustn't exceed the rating of the Scenario or Adventure they are posted on. Direct or indirect mentions of a higher rating or higher-rated content in a Scenario or Adventure will automatically cause it to be that rating. In Multiple Choice Scenarios, the highest rated choice determines the overall Scenario rating.

Private and Unlisted Scenarios and Adventures made for personal use are not moderated and therefore not subjected to these guidelines. Instead, our [Walls Approach](https://latitude.io/blog/the-walls-approach) applies, preventing any content suggesting, promoting, or glorifying the sexual exploitation of children.

# **Content Ratings**

Content ratings do not limit what can happen in an Adventure — If you wish to restrict what kind of content can be generated in your Adventure, please refer to our [AI Safety Settings](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/what-are-the-ai-safety-settings).

## **Everyone (E)**

### **Content suitable for all ages, comparable with: ESRB E10+, MPAA PG or PEGI 7+**

**Violence and Danger:** Fantasy or cartoon style violence that doesn't depict realistic injury or consequences. Peril or tension where the stakes are low. Competitive conflict.

-   Pirates sword fighting where the emphasis is on sinking an enemy ship or capturing (not killing) the enemy captain and no major injury is depicted.
-   Defeating a dragon.
-   Escaping a mildly scary monster in a forest.
-   A race between characters.
-   Two knights testing their skills in a tournament.
-   Academic competition between friends or classmates.
-   Spirits interacting in friendly or mischievous ways.
-   Someone jumping out of a hiding spot to scare another causing only mild shock.
-   Characters interacting in friendly, helpful, or professional ways.

**Romance and Relationships:** Simple courtship. Innocent crushes and friendship. Brief friendly contact.

-   Princess and hero falling in love through adventure.
-   Characters sharing a sweet first dance.
-   Friends developing innocent crushes.
-   Simple "true love's kiss" in fairy tale context.
-   No romantic/attraction elements between authority figures and subordinates; relationships strictly appropriate (teacher guiding student in academics, coach mentoring athlete in sports skills).
-   Characters helping vulnerable others with purely supportive intentions; rescue scenarios with no romantic elements; friendship offered during difficult circumstances.

**Language and Presentation:** Mild language (no profanity) especially when shown as an area for personal growth.

-   Playground insults and teasing between friends.
-   Light-hearted banter between characters.

**Themes and Subject Matter:** Simple moral dilemmas with clearly understood right and wrong. Fun, imagination-filled adventures and fantasy worlds. Content appropriate for pre-teens with straightforward themes. Characters with clear, understandable motivations without complex psychological aspects.

-   A story about friendly vampires helping their community.
-   Characters with supernatural powers used for everyday tasks.
-   Fantasy creatures living normal lives.
-   A character feeling sad about moving to a new town.
-   Friends having a disagreement but working it out.
-   Someone learning to cope with stage fright.
-   Limited influence abilities raising no ethical questions; control is over inanimate objects; no harm or sexual implications.

## **Teen (T)**

### Content suitable for readers aged 13 and above, comparable with: ESRB T, MPAA PG-13 or PEGI 12+

**Violence and Danger:** Descriptions of violence where death is possible, injury is depicted, but without gore or excessive detail. Intense peril or thriller tension where there's still a chance for survival or hope for positive resolution.

-   Adventure stories with fantasy violence and peril. Examples: "Lord of the Rings", "The Hobbit", "The Chronicles of Narnia", "The Dark Tower"
-   Superhero stories with stylized action and clear good vs evil. Examples: "The Avengers", "The Dark Knight"

**Romance and Relationships:** First romance and dating themes. Flirting and attraction without explicit sexual content. Physical affection appropriate in public settings. Relationship conflicts with low emotional stakes. Mentions of romantic relationships without explicit content. Relationship difficulties that don't involve deep psychological exploration.

-   High school drama with themes of first relationships. Examples: "The Breakfast Club", "Mean Girls", "10 Things I Hate About You"
-   Romantic comedies focused on the development of a relationship. Examples: "The Proposal", "Crazy, Stupid, Love", "The Break-Up"
-   A couple living together experiencing distance in their relationship without detailed psychological exploration.
-   A character noticing their partner seems distracted or less interested in the relationship.
-   Mild arguments or tension between partners that don't involve manipulation or abuse.
-   Mentions of cohabitation or sharing living spaces without sexual focus.
-   Simple relationship problems like growing apart, loss of interest, communication issues.
-   Brief, non-explicit mentions of physical attraction or appearance.
-   Mild jealousy, wandering eyes, or interest in others without detailed exploration.
-   Relationships with characters described as "vindictive" or "spiteful" without detailed toxic behavior.
-   Use of terms like "lover" when context suggests emotional rather than explicitly sexual meaning.
-   Mild attraction between characters with minimal authority differences (volunteer coordinator and a new volunteer, senior and junior students); clear boundaries maintained; no power exploitation.
-   Temporary vulnerability with appropriate support; mild attraction developing after crisis resolved; recognition of vulnerability without exploitation.

**Language and Presentation:** Single instance of strong language (non-sexual variants only). Moderate use of cursing and vulgar language.

-   Films with occasional strong language like "The Martian" or "The Breakfast Club"
-   Content with expressions like "damn," "hell," or occasional use of stronger words.
-   Coming-of-age stories with realistic teenage dialogue.
-   Stories that include mild insults or verbal confrontations between characters.

**Themes and Subject Matter:** More complex social dynamics appropriate for early teens. Social drama and mild conflict with relatable stakes. Characters with nuanced motivations while maintaining clear overall moral frameworks. Psychological themes that introduce complexity but remain accessible and not focused on violence or sexuality. References to personal struggles with identity, belonging, or purpose without extensive psychological depth. Supernatural powers or abilities that raise ethical questions but don't explore deep psychological manipulation. Unsettling encounters or mild boundary violations that create tension without severe psychological distress. References to concepts that could be associated with fetishes, even when not explicitly sexualized or the central focus.

-   High school drama with themes of bullying.
-   The character of "Thanos" from Marvel Comics who is a supervillain whose motivations are complex and, in his view, justified.
-   Mystery stories with suspense and danger.
-   Stories addressing social issues through metaphor or allegory.
-   Exploration of fame, social pressure, or public scrutiny when presented in a straightforward manner.
-   Characters who gain supernatural abilities and explore basic ethical questions about using them.
-   Brief unsettling encounters with strangers that create discomfort but not severe distress.
-   A character discovering they can influence others in minor ways through supernatural means.
-   References to cosplay, uniforms, or distinctive clothing that might have fetish associations, but presented in a non-sexual context.
-   Characters transformed into unusual forms where the focus is on adaptation and survival rather than graphic detail or psychological trauma.
-   Limited influence abilities raising ethical questions; control is temporary and targets can resist; no lasting harm or sexual implications.

**Content that automatically increases the rating to at least Teen**

-   Single instance of strong language (non-sexual variants only).
-   Mild flirtation and romantic attraction between characters.
-   Brief descriptions of physical attractiveness that aren't explicitly sexual.
-   References to personal struggles or identity that don't deeply explore trauma.
-   Unsettling or creepy content without explicit threat or harm.
-   Supernatural powers with moral implications that don't involve deep psychological manipulation.

## **Mature (M)**

### Content suitable for readers aged 17 and above. Comparable with: ESRB M, MPAA R or PEGI 18+

**Violence and Danger:** Detailed action sequences with serious blood, death, and injury without glamorizing or romanticizing gore. Intense peril and danger where the stakes are high and the potential for death is present. Content that emphasizes mortality and lethal consequences, even without graphic descriptions. Scenarios where the primary abilities or powers are centered around causing death.

-   Mass deaths, murder, serial killings, etc.
-   Crime dramas with graphic violence or disturbing content. Examples: "The Silence of the Lambs", "The Godfather"
-   War narratives with realistic combat and casualties. Examples: "All Quiet on the Western Front", "Saving Private Ryan", "The Hurt Locker"
-   Stories with slavery, concentration camps, or other forms of institutionalized abuse. Examples: "Schindler's List", "The Book Thief", "The Diary of Anne Frank", "Gladiator"
-   Content featuring instant-death abilities or unavoidable lethal consequences.

**Romance and Relationships:** Sexual or romantic tension that implies potential sexual activity. Complex adult relationship dynamics. Relationship themes involving betrayal or infidelity. Brief sexual content (when not the primary focus). Explicit discussions of attraction or desire. Relationship content requiring significant emotional maturity to process. Premises that establish one character with potential romantic/sexual access to multiple others. Scenarios involving pursuing multiple romantic interests or common romance visual novel frameworks.

-   Multiple women attempting to date a single man or multiple men attempting to date a single woman.
-   Toxic relationship dynamics showing manipulation or emotional abuse (while clearly showing these as negative).
-   Stories dealing with domestic abuse or toxic relationships without romanticizing abuse.
-   Romantic comedies or dramas with themes of infidelity, sexual tension, and emotional manipulation. Examples: "Lost in Translation", "No Strings Attached", "Silver Linings Playbook", "Call Me by Your Name"
-   A relationship where one partner is systematically manipulating or gaslighting the other.
-   Detailed psychological exploration of a character's declining mental health due to relationship issues.
-   Stories centered on infidelity with explicit descriptions of the affair.
-   "Bedroom adjacent" scenes where sexual activity is implied but not described.
-   Non-traditional relationship structures (polyamory, open relationships).
-   Detailed or prolonged descriptions of a character's body and sexual features.
-   Detailed exploration of relationship breakdown with psychological depth.
-   Severe toxic relationship dynamics featuring explicit emotional abuse.
-   Detailed exploration of authority or influence disparities in relationships where one party has significant control over the other.
-   Complex psychological elements of falling out of love.
-   A lone male character in an all-female environment (or vice versa) where romantic potential is implied.
-   "Fish out of water" (meaning an unfamiliar or uncomfortable situation where someone feels out of place) scenarios where gender imbalance is a central focus.
-   Significant authority differences acknowledged as ethically complex; relationships that begin after formal authority roles end; explicit discussion of power imbalance concerns.
-   Complex portrayal of relationships involving vulnerability; consent concerns explicitly addressed between characters; mutual support through genuine hardship.

**Language and Presentation:** Multiple instances of strong language. Use of some sexual variants of cursing.

-   Content with frequent use of harsh language like "The Wolf of Wall Street" or "Deadpool"
-   Stories that include sexual references in dialogue.
-   Narratives involving coarse language in realistic contexts like military settings or crime stories.
-   Content that features creative or excessive use of profanity for comic or dramatic effect.

**Themes and Subject Matter:** Dark themes with purpose and meaningful exploration. Significant moral ambiguity requiring adult judgment. Complex moral/psychological themes that challenge conventional thinking. Content requiring emotional maturity to process appropriately. Detailed exploration of psychological trauma, addiction, or mental health issues. Deep examination of existential questions or identity crises. Powers or abilities that explicitly involve deep psychological manipulation of others. Encounters that cause deep psychological distress. Scenarios where characters cannot distinguish reality from fantasy, causing deep psychological distress. Scenarios where a character's embarrassment is clearly meant to be arousing or titillating.

-   Narratives exploring substance abuse and addiction.
-   Stories featuring disturbing content like body horror or psychological distress.
-   Characters dealing with serious mental health issues like depression or anxiety.
-   Complex family conflicts involving emotional manipulation.
-   Revenge plots with dark themes and moral ambiguity.
-   Stories dealing with deliberate institutional cover-ups resulting in civilian casualties.
-   Scenarios dealing with survival amid institutional collapse or betrayal of workplace/institutional trust.
-   A character who can control others' dreams and uses this power to psychologically torment them.
-   Powers that allow violation of others' mental privacy or autonomy in significant ways.
-   Forced physical contact that creates severe psychological trauma.
-   Scenarios where characters cannot distinguish reality from fantasy, causing deep psychological distress.
-   Scenarios where a character's embarrassment is clearly meant to be arousing or titillating.
-   Significant control causing psychological distress; complex moral exploration; no sexual applications.

**Content that automatically increases the rating to at least Mature**

-   Non-traditional relationship structures (polyamory, open relationships).
-   Sexual themes (when secondary to plot).
-   Multiple instances of strong language.
-   Severe toxic relationship dynamics (featuring explicit emotional abuse, manipulation, or gaslighting).
-   Detailed descriptions of physical attraction with clear sexual undertones.
-   Powers or abilities explicitly used to control or manipulate others psychologically (may not be used for sexual purposes).
-   Scenarios where embarrassment has clear sexual undertones or is eroticized.
-   Scenarios where the simultaneous pursuit of or being pursued by multiple romantic/sexual interests within a single storyline is presented as a central goal or motivation (distinct from scenarios that simply offer a choice of different characters/paths at the beginning).
-   References to nudity in non-sexual contexts (medical settings, changing rooms) where modesty is maintained.
-   Brief implied nudity where characters take appropriate measures to maintain privacy.
-   Any mention of succubus or incubus as these are sexual beings by nature.
-   Content that explicitly instructs or encourages unforgiving gameplay where death is emphasized.
-   Scenarios including body horror without explicit descriptions.
-   The use of ecchi as a theme or tag.

**Romance and Relationships:** Sexual content as primary focus (consensual). Content focused on sexual fetishes, kinks, BDSM. Glorifying or romanticizing behaviors where one person manipulates, coerces, or controls another.

-   Content with extremely graphic language that is sexual in nature.
-   Stories where provocative language is central to the narrative rather than incidental.
-   Content that employs language deliberately designed to elicit shock, disgust, or as a tool to disturb or provoke.
-   Narratives where sexually explicit dialogue dominates interactions between characters.

**Themes and Subject Matter:** Extreme psychological elements designed primarily to disturb or provoke. Systematic removal of character agency as a central theme. Disturbing content explicitly meant to provoke shock, disgust, or severe psychological discomfort. Themes that deliberately push ethical boundaries to their limits.

-   Dark psychological themes that don't promote harm, such as explorations of human psychological breaking points.
-   Content designed to disturb while maintaining ethical boundaries, such as extreme dystopian scenarios where characters face impossible moral choices.

-   Horror scenarios that focus on psychological torture or manipulation.

# Image Content

When you upload a custom cover image, our image rating system reviews it and assigns it a content rating (Everyone, Teen, Mature, Unrated) using the same scale as the rest of these Community Guidelines. Two things determine that rating: the visual content it depicts and any text found inside the image. Your image's final rating is the most restrictive rating triggered by either one, and some content will cause the image to be marked Unpublishable, meaning it can't be used on published content.

## **Visual Content**

Regardless of any text, the visual content of your image affects its rating. Our system checks your image for the following general categories of content:

-   **Nudity and sexual content** - nudity, sexual activity, sexualized poses or behavior, sexual displays, sex toys, and suggestive content.
-   **Revealing clothing** - tight-fitting clothing, undergarments, swimwear, and shirtless figures.
-   **Violence and gore** - fighting, blood, injuries, graphic gore, corpses, and disturbing body imagery.
-   **Weapons** - guns, knives, and other weapons.
-   **Drugs, alcohol, and smoking** - alcohol, smoking, pills, marijuana, and injectable drugs.
-   **Self-harm** - self-harm imagery, such as hanging or nooses.
-   **Hate and extremist symbols** - imagery associated with hate groups or extremist ideologies.
-   **Obscene gestures**.
-   **Animal abuse and animal genitalia**.

Most flagged content works on a sliding scale: the clearer, more prominent, or more explicit it is, the higher the rating it earns, and your image is always assigned the highest rating that any single element reaches. Ratings are also based on what an image appears to depict or hints at, not only what it literally shows. Content that is framed, cropped, obscured, or styled to imply higher-rated material can be rated as though that material were present.

## **Visual Content That Maxes Out at Teen**

If an image contains only the content below, the rating for it maxes out at Teen, no matter how prominent that content is. Other content detected in the same image can still push the final rating higher:

-   Shirtless men
-   Alcohol, drinking, and smoking
-   Pills
-   Miniskirts

## **Visual Content That Maxes Out at Mature**

If an image contains only the content below, the rating for it maxes out at Mature, no matter how prominent that content is. Other content detected in the same image can still push the final rating higher:

-   Tight-fitting athletic wear

## **Visual Content That Starts at Teen and can Raise the Rating**

Everything below starts at Teen when it is mild or ambiguous and climbs to Mature and Unrated as it becomes clearer, more explicit, or more graphic. Pushed far enough, some of this content crosses into imagery that is never allowed on a cover image at all - see **Custom Cover Images** under the Unpublishable section:

-   **Suggestive and sexual content** - suggestive posing or framing, tight-fitting clothing, undergarments, and swimwear. Visible undergarments reach Mature almost immediately, and sexual content escalates quickly.
-   **Violence and gore** - fighting, blood, and injuries start low; heavier gore and corpses push ratings to the higher end. Animal cruelty is treated the same way.

## **Visual Content That Does Not Raise the Rating**

On their own, the following content is detected but is ignored and won't raise your image above Everyone. Other content in the same image can still set a higher rating:

-   Real guns or knives simply shown (held, in hand, or at rest)
-   Clearly cartoonish or stylized corpses

## **Restricted Words in Images**

If your image contains text (captions, watermarks, speech bubbles, logos, and so on), we scan that text for restricted words. When a restricted word is found, the image is assigned a rating based on the severity of that word, and your image's final rating can never be lower than that.

-   **Explicit sexual terminology** - Anatomical terms, sexual acts, sexual slang, and pornographic terminology.
-   **Racial and ethnic slurs** - Slurs and derogatory terms targeting racial, ethnic, or national groups.
-   **Profanity and vulgar language** - Strong profanity, crude insults, and vulgar slang.
-   **Pornographic references** - Names of pornographic websites, genres, or industry-specific terminology.
-   **Hate group references** - Terms associated with hate groups, extremist ideologies, or hate symbols.
-   **Fetish and paraphilia terminology** - Terms for specific sexual fetishes, paraphilias, or extreme sexual practices.
-   **Drug-related terms** - Specific drug names
-   **Self-harm references** - Terms directly referencing suicide or self-harm methods

# **Unpublishable Content**

### Do not publish or post any content which depicts, encourages, promotes, alludes to, or otherwise contains any of the themes listed below.

-   **Spam, Plagiarism, and Content Manipulation** — Identical or near-identical content being repeatedly published, Posting / Publishing another user’s content that is not your own, attempts to manipulate content or votes, or impersonation of individuals or organizations on or off of Latitude’s platforms — especially in regard to individuals or organizations affiliated with Latitude, Inc. and/or with the intent of deceiving or causing harm to anyone.
-   **Professional Advice** — Content professing the ability to provide, professional, financial, legal, medical, or mental health advice / therapy. AI Dungeon has **NOT** been calibrated or trained as a tool capable of this and if you choose to leverage text returned by the AI within the AI Dungeon experience for any of these purposes, you do so at your own risk. Latitude, Inc. cannot be held responsible for actions taken in line with recommendations provided by the AI. You are encouraged to seek the counsel / advice of actual industry professionals — **AI Dungeon is a game experience.**
-   **External links** — If you wish to include an external link anywhere, you must get approval by emailing it to [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com) first.
-   **Hateful Content** — Hate speech or discrimination against other people based on attributes such as their race, ethnicity, national origin, sex, gender, sexual orientation, religious affiliation, or disabilities (Fantastical depictions of fictional discrimination, like Dwarves loathing Elves, are exempt).
-   **Glorifying real-world violence or tragedies** — Recent and ongoing events can be hot topic issues, please use discretion when creating scenarios based on these events. Exploring Alt-History is acceptable as long as it doesn’t promote any content that could be considered to be hateful content.
-   **Glorifying or promoting self harm or suicide** — If you are experiencing distress and are considering self harm, help is available by texting or calling the [Suicide and Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) at `988` (US only, [international numbers can be found here](https://blog.opencounseling.com/suicide-hotlines/)).
-   **Harmful content about real people, other users or public personas** — Inciting or promoting violence or abuse against others, unauthorized sharing of personal information (also known as “doxxing”), sexualizing or sexual harassment, or threatening behavior, including threatening another’s life, safety, reputation, livelihood, or well-being.
-   **Bestiality** — Sexual relations with or attraction towards below human intelligence, non-consenting or entirely animal bodied species (A centaur would be considered to have human level intelligence and only a partial animal body, while a horse or Pokémon would not).
-   **Necrophilia** — Sexual intercourse with or attraction towards corpses or below human intelligence, non-consenting, or rotting undead (A Vampire would be considered to have human intelligence and a non-rotting body, while a Zombie would not).
-   **Sexual content involving minors** — Any exploitation or sexualization of real or fictional minors, including high schoolers and established underage characters from media, or sexual content implying characters are underage despite disclaimers ("All characters are 18+" when context implies otherwise. For example in a high school setting). This also includes sexual descriptions of characters presented as children, such as characters labeled “loli” or “shota” or described with a childlike body, no matter what age, species, or series they are given.
-   **Series built on sexualizing minors** — A small number of series are built around sexualizing a cast of high schoolers or younger characters. Scenarios based on these series are Unpublishable, even if the scenario itself contains no sexual content, because the source material steers play toward that content. A series falls into this group when both of these are true:

-   Its main characters are in high school or younger, or under 18 in the original work (Including 18 year olds in high school).
-   Sexualizing them is the point of the series, not a single moment in it. For example, the premise or the main character’s goal is sexual, fan service recurs through most of the series, its powers or plot are built around it, or it is known as an ecchi series.

Examples include High School DxD and To Love-Ru. Series that simply have school-age characters, such as My Hero Academia or Persona, are not in this group, but sexual content involving their underage characters is still Unpublishable under our guidelines.

-   **Incest** — Sexual interactions between family members or close relatives, including romantic or sexual relationships, clear flirtatious behavior, sexualized descriptions of family members or intimate physical contact (Step-family relations are treated the same as blood relations).
-   **Non-consensual sexual content and sexual violence** — The capability to consent needs to be clearly demonstrated within the context of the story and cannot be assumed. If a character does not have control or is coerced into a sexual situation, it will be considered a non-consensual sexual act. Examples of non-consensual sexual acts include:

-   Rape, sexual assault, sex slavery or trafficking.
-   Sexual situations tied to violence, abduction, intoxication, mind control, hypnosis, or other magical manipulation (such as spells, potions, etc.).
-   Situations where a character's survival depends on performing sexual acts, or where sexual offers are made under extreme duress.
-   Abuse of power, such as a boss coercing an employee, or law enforcement exchanging leniency for sexual favors.

Disclaimers of any kind do not absolve content from being Unpublishable if it violates any of the Unpublishable restrictions above.

## Custom Cover Images

### While the above guidelines apply to images, there are some additional guidelines specific to them. **Do not upload images that include the following content:**

-   **Pornographic Imagery and Sexual Content** — Any explicit depiction of sexual acts, fetishes, nudity, use of sex toys or other objects in a sexual context, animal genitalia, overly suggestive poses and outfits, other imagery intended to be provocative or for the purpose of sexual arousal or gratification, or where text, logos, watermarks, or graphic elements appear to be deliberately positioned to cover or obscure sexually suggestive areas of a character's body.
-   **Violence and Shock Content** — Images depicting extreme violence, cruelty, or harm to individuals or animals. Graphically violent or gory content, including heavy gore, corpses, or emaciated bodies, that may be disturbing or shocking. Imagery designed to provoke discomfort or distress through its violent nature.
-   **Self-Harm and Suicide Imagery** - Images that depict, glorify, or promote self-harm or suicide, including depictions such as hanging or nooses.
-   **Hate and Extremist Symbols** - Hate symbols or imagery associated with hate groups or extremist ideologies (for example, Nazi, KKK, terrorist, or Confederate symbols).
-   **Vulgar and Crude Content** — Use of vulgar, offensive, or crude language in images or imagery containing obscene gestures or actions.
-   **Misleading Imagery (Clickbait)** — Images designed to mislead players into believing the content is something other than what it actually is or any form of deceptive imagery that tricks users into engaging with content.
-   **Underage Characters in Suggestive Scenarios** — Images depicting characters who appear to be minors (under 18 years old) will be treated as such and may not appear on custom cover images for sexually suggestive Scenarios and Adventures.

# **Enforcement**

Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, any combination of the following actions, with or without notice, may occur:

-   Deleting the offending Comment.
-   Removing the offending Cover image.
-   Rerating and locking the rating of the offending Scenario or Adventure.
-   Blocking the publishing of a Scenario or Adventure that violates our guidelines.
-   Temporary or permanent restriction of an offending user’s ability to publish content.

Users may petition for the removal of a publishing restriction or wait until the expiration of a temporary restriction period. Attempting to circumvent a publishing restriction, or causing particularly egregious or frequent violations, may be met with a total platform ban.
