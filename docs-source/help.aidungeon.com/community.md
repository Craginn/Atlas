# AI Dungeon Community

## Join us on Discord

![icon](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/49a9bd40-0208-445e-a86e-5149e1b2be50/ic_round-discord.svg)

Come hang out with other players and our devs on [the official AI Dungeon Discord](https://aidungeon.com/discord)!

-   Meet people in the community
-   Ask questions or get help from our Staff and Teams
-   Look for other players to join a Multiplayer game
-   Share your creations and get feedback
-   Read tips and tricks for getting the most out of AI Dungeon
-   Join conversations and learn more about Voyage

**Our Discord server has several channels you might be interested in:**

-   **#ai-dungeon-discussion** is the primary place players discuss all things AI Dungeon; ask questions, pitch ideas, or strike up an AI Dungeon-related conversation with a fellow player!
-   **#community-help** is a great channel for asking quick questions about playing AI Dungeon and getting support from other players around the world.
-   **[#bugs-feature-requests](https://discord.gg/RcYntV44fF)** is the number one spot to post about and submit bugs or feature requests related to AI Dungeon. Before posting feedback or a new bug report, please search the forum to see if a similar post already exists.

## FAQs

### **Can I be part of the AI Dungeon community even if I don't publish my content?**

Of course! If you're an AI Dungeon player, then you're a member of the community by default. We welcome active members and lurkers alike. If you're hoping to engage with more players, then we encourage you to [join our Discord server](https://aidungeon.com/discord)! You can also follow us on [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/aidungeon), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/aidungeon/), [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/latitude_official), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/aidungeon).

### ****Where can I watch others playing AI Dungeon?****

[We created a playlist on our YouTube channel featuring people playing AI Dungeon—check it out!](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5Unfkaz5UH1K3Cvt9FzCur-KBSpE4Zqc) You can also search for fellow AI Dungeon players on Twitch and YouTube—there are tons out there. Our friends at [Secret Sleepover Society](https://www.twitch.tv/secretsleepoversociety) sometimes play AI Dungeon on their streams. We love their adventures!

### Do you support Multiplayer?

Yes we do! [Read our article about Multiplayer mode →](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/do-you-support-multiplayer)
