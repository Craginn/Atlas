# How do you submit Feedback IDs?

If you’ve experienced an unusual AI response and would like to help our team diagnose the issue, you can do so by submitting a Feedback ID. To get a Feedback ID from your Adventure, simply follow these steps:

1.  Click on the last AI response and then on `REPORT AN ISSUE`
2.  Describe the issue in the text box before clicking `SUBMIT FEEDBACK`
3.  Copy the Feedback ID.

1.  You can either send this Feedback ID to our [Discord](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/is-there-an-ai-dungeon-discord-server) in a Bug Report form, or send it in an email to [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com)

Reporting an issue in this way will provide a snippet of your adventure, context, and setup to developers for investigation. This information is anonymous and is never shared or sold anywhere else, nor is it used for anything beyond bug diagnosis.

Thank you for improving AI Dungeon for everyone!
