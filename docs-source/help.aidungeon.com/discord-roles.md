# Apply for a Discord Role

The AI Dungeon Discord server has several special roles available:

-   Moderators—Help enforce the community guidelines in the AI Dungeon Discord server.
-   Helpers—Knowledgeable AI Dungeon users who help others learn how to play AI Dungeon.
-   Creators—Our top creators from AI Dungeon and Voyage.
-   Community Quality Assurance—Bug testers who test early-access features and help keep the platform stable by reporting issues to developers.

If you’re interested in having one of these roles, please follow the instructions below:

## Moderator

### **Qualifications**

Our moderators are our front line of defense in maintaining the safety of our Discord community. They take actions such as warning, muting, and even banning players. They also help make sure discussion stays on topic for each channel, and act as a liaison between the community and the Latitude Team.

As such, moderators are expected to conduct themselves professionally. They set the tone for community interactions.

Previous experience with moderation is helpful but not required.

Extensive knowledge of AI Dungeon is also helpful, but not required.

Moderators must be 18 years or older.

### **How to Apply**

To begin the process, please email [community@latitude.io](mailto:community@latitude.io) and include the following information:

-   Name and pronouns we can call you by
-   Email address used with AI Dungeon
-   Discord handle
-   Reddit username
-   Answers to the following two questions:

-   Why you want to be on the moderation staff?
-   What’s one suggestion for improving our Discord server and community?

Once we’ve received your application, a member of our Community Team will reach out to you on Discord to continue the process. We will ask a series of questions, and you’ll be invited to do either a text or audio interview. The text interview option would take place on Discord where our Community Team and Senior Moderators will ask you questions asynchronously over the course of a few days. Or you can opt for an audio call with a member of our Community Team.

If accepted, you’ll be granted a Trial Moderator role, where we can see you in action. If the team is comfortable with your understanding and application of the server rules, you’ll be added as a Moderator on the team.

## Helper

### Responsibilities

Our helpers are knowledgeable players in the community. Many of them have been playing for as long as AI Dungeon has been around. Some of them are AI aficionados, and they’re able to describe the nuances of constructing a great AI-driven Adventure.

Helpers are also knowledgeable about the creation process on AI Dungeon. They may know a bit about some of the pesky bugs and have workarounds to suggest to players as well. They have a great ability and interest in helping all players increase their skills and knowledge in AI Dungeon.

Helpers don’t have to know everything, but they should be excited to continue learning about AI Dungeon.

### How to Apply

To apply, please send an email to our Community Team at [community@latitude.io](mailto:community@latitude.io) and include the following information:

-   Name and pronouns we can call you by
-   Email address used with AI Dungeon
-   Discord handle
-   Tell us why you’d like to be a Helper and a bit about your experience using AI Dungeon

Once we’ve received your application, a member of our Community Team will reach out to you on Discord to continue the process.

## Creator

### About Creators

Our creator community builds Scenarios on AI Dungeon, and they are a critical part of our ecosystem and community. With the Creator Discord roles, we want to provide greater visibility to our creators, as well as provide them inside access to the Latitude Team.

There are 3 Creator roles (criteria to qualify down below) and one special Creator role:

-   Top Creator
-   Creator
-   Emerging Creator
-   Community Creator

### Earning the Creator Role

If your account qualifies for a Creator role and you have connected your account to Discord in your settings, then you will automatically receive the Discord role. If you need any help, please email [support@aidungeon.com](mailto:support@aidungeon.com).

Creator status is evaluated every 3 months at the beginning of every quarter. The Creator role is not permanent and must be maintained.

**Top Creator**

-   250,000 actions in the previous quarter
-   Account must be 3+ months old
-   Must publish at least 3 new Scenarios in the previous quarter

**Creator**

-   50,000 actions in the previous quarter
-   Account must be 3+ months old
-   Must publish at least 2 new Scenarios in the previous quarter

**Emerging Creator**

-   25,000 actions in the previous quarter
-   Account must be less than 6 months old
-   Must publish at least 2 new Scenarios in the previous quarter

### Community Creator

Community Creator is a special role exclusive to our Discord. It's handpicked by our team and goes to people who make things that help other players get more out of AI Dungeon and Voyage, such as scripts, AI Instructions, browser extensions, tools, and guides. Scenarios and worlds have their own Creator roles, so this one is for everything built around them.

This role is to reward those that maybe don’t make scenarios or content on AI Dungeon, but are still providing value to players in another way, such as creating Scripts, making AI Instructions, browser extensions, and so on.

We pick new Community Creators every quarter, like the Creator roles. Like those roles, it isn't permanent: to keep it, you need to keep creating.

## Community Quality Assurance (CQA)

CQA are members of our community who dedicate their time to finding and documenting bugs, assisting the development team in improving the game, and fixing it when it breaks. They have direct contact with developers, and may occasionally be seen asking questions about bugs you've encountered.

If you're interested in being a part of CQA, please [fill out this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfjE8P-lrfOlnCoD00yZ1LBFUfLMtxdIqbpkA2CWbip7UNGYw/viewform?usp=header)!
