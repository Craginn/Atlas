# Getting Started

> Welcome to AI Dungeon, an AI-guided role play experience and interactive storytelling tool. [Create your account now](https://play.aidungeon.com/create-account)!

Join [our official Discord server](https://aidungeon.com/discord) to find a community of players who are incredible at answering questions and providing insight to the game! The AI Dungeon team and devs also interact frequently and post often.

## The Basics

### Choose Your Adventure

[Quick Start](https://play.aidungeon.com/scenario/1r0T3TXT/new-adventure/play) a new game using the Play button and choose a setting, character, and name, then watch as the AI fills in details for your Adventure.

Or, pick from hundreds of community-published, predefined Scenarios to start your Adventure. The [Discover](https://play.aidungeon.com/discover) section is a great place to explore and find content you want to play.

### **Take A Turn**

This is a roleplay experience, so Take a Turn giving your character [Do](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/do-mode) or [Say](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/say-mode) actions. Or, write your own text with [Story](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/story-mode) to move the plot along how you want. It’s your Adventure: anything you can type, you can do!

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/8e65fa5e-075a-4dd4-b5b2-b5745e3d7adf/TAT_1.gif?width=1000)

The AI will generate responses from your inputs and can advance the story to introduce characters, world events, and other elements for you to interact with during gameplay.

Every story is unique and unexpected.

### **Make it Yours**

You are the main character _and_ director of your story.

Use Undo, Redo, Retry, and Edit to control the action. Use [Plot Components](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/plot-components) and [Story Cards](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/story-cards) to keep the AI coherent and consistent. 

Live out your wild ideas however you want: you’re in charge!

## Things You Should Know

1.  **Anything is possible.** Literally anything. You can type any action you think of and the AI will try to generate a story off of your inputs. It's not always perfect, but you'll be surprised at what's possible.
2.  **AI is hard.** This is a game unlike anything you've ever played before: it uses cutting edge AI technology to generate responses. That being said, there may be weird quirks that you’ll notice. We're always working on improving the AI and keeping up with research, so AI Dungeon will get better as new AI advancements come out.
3.  **Use the tools.** There are a lot of ways you can improve your gameplay when the AI struggles. Retrying or Editing bad outputs helps the AI pick up on good patterns to mimic instead of bad ones. Using features like Memory and Story Cards helps keep important information at the forefront of the AI’s context so it can generate new text based on the most relevant details.
4.  **Revisit your content.** Your Adventures and Scenarios are automatically saved and can be rejoined or edited at any time by visiting them in “My Stuff”.

### **How does AI Dungeon even work?**

It's part-science, part-magic. ✨

Just kidding, it’s math. So much math. More math than the human brain can comprehend or fathom.

AI Dungeon uses a type of artificial intelligence called a neural net to mathematically analyze the text you put in, then generate text that is likely to follow. Basically, the AI ‘reads’ the last part of your story and tries to guess the next possible set of words, generating the story and results of your actions as you play in endless virtual worlds.

**[Read this blog post for more information →](https://latitude.io/blog/ai-magic-what-it-takes-to-play-ai-games)**

### **Why do I need an account to play?**

We host the AI models on the cloud to enable seamless play on any web browser or mobile device of your choosing.

We also store your adventures in the cloud, so you can always access and play them through your account, even if you switch between devices. You access them through “My Stuff”, which is linked to your account. And, if you choose to publish your stories, they will be listed under your account name.

![image](https://assets.super.so/311da5e0-9a02-4177-9f6a-b89435645aea/images/d08ed2ae-2efe-4c6c-95cb-314c4f544a4f/Untitled.png?width=1600)

In short, having an activated account on AI Dungeon lets us give you a full experience and lets you keep your adventures safe.

### **Can I play AI Dungeon on my phone?**

Yes, you can! We want to make sure players can enjoy AI Dungeon whether they're on the go or only have access through a mobile device.

You can play AI Dungeon on your phone or other mobile devices by downloading our app:

**[Google Play Store →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aidungeon&hl=en&gl=US)**

**[Apple App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-dungeon/id1491268416)**

You can also play on our website through your mobile browser at [play.aidungeon.com](https://play.aidungeon.com/), just like you would on a desktop computer, if the app isn’t working for any reason.

### **Can I play AI Dungeon for free?**

**Yes, AI Dungeon is a free-to-play game, no matter what device you're playing on.** Your free account grants you access to all of AI Dungeon's basic features.

If you're curious about [additional features available to Premium subscribers](https://help.aidungeon.com/memberships-benefits), check out AI Dungeon subscriptions. **Free trials are available.**
