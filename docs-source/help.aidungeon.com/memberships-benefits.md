### More Context

We [recently](https://aidungeon.com/renaissance) doubled the context sizes offered in AI Dungeon. And then [we did it again](https://aidungeon.com/aura). Context lengths are aligned with each tier. Free gets up to 4k tokens of context, Journey gets up to 8k, Legend gets up to 16k, and Mythic and Ultimate gets up to 32k. Subscribed players can also gain larger context lengths by spending 1 credit per action for a set amount of context tokens (only applicable for some models).

Context length determines how much text is sent to the AI model to generate the next action in an AI Dungeon Adventure. When you take an action, your Adventure text is combined with any information stored in the AI Instructions, Story Summary, Plot Essentials, Author’s Notes, and relevant Story Cards. If you’re using a shorter context length, the beginning of your Adventure will likely be trimmed, or Story Cards may be removed in order to fit in the context window.

By increasing the context length, you are increasing the amount of your Adventure (and additional details like Plot Essentials and Story Cards) the AI can receive and consider for your next action. More context can help improve the quality and coherency of Adventures, especially as they get longer.

## FAQs

### What happened to the ‘Hero’, ‘Adventurer’, and ‘Champion’ tier?

When we deprecated the Hero tier, we were working on a brand new platform called [Heroes](https://blog.latitude.io/heroes-dev-logs). We anticipated that the naming of Heroes would be confusing with our mid-level ($15/month) subscription tier, Hero.

Because of this, we renamed the Hero tier to ‘Champion’.

We’ve now since switched the name of that platform to [Voyage](https://help.aidungeon.com/voyage-access), meaning that this deprecation ended up not mattering much.

In the end, we settled on the name **Journey** instead.

If you were subscribed to the **Hero** or **Champion** plan, there is no action necessary for you to transition to the **Journey** plan. Nothing else about the tier has changed.

In the case of **Adventurer**, it’s a different story. When you have subscription tiers that go up incrementally in price, you expect to see a curve of distribution; more people should realistically be subscribed to the lowest tier, and less as you go up in price.

However, we found that many of our users preferred to subscribe to Journey over Adventurer, despite Adventurer being the cheapest tier. This is likely because the Adventurer tier was priced awkwardly, sitting at only 5 dollars a month less than Journey, but having half of the features and context that Journey receives. We figured that rather than lessening Journey’s benefits or lowering/raising the price arbitrarily, we would simply give Free players what Adventurer had, and deprecate the tier.

However, we will continue to provide support for it for as long as we still have subscribers. **If you are or were subscribed to the Adventurer tier**, you will continue to stay subscribed to it without any effort needed on your part, so long as you don’t switch or change your subscription.

### Why is the ‘Ultimate’ tier so expensive?

For those of you who have wanted to play with the newest, most powerful models at the largest context sizes available, we introduced a larger subscription tier, Ultimate. This new tier gives players access to our most powerful AI models and context lengths up to 32k and beyond when using Credits.

In the past, we’ve shied away from offering more powerful models and extreme context lengths because there wasn’t a way to offer them to you without putting ourselves out of business. **Powerful models (even with only 2k context length) are up to 120x more expensive than models we usually offer on our Free tier.** Offering models with larger context lengths dramatically increases AI costs, as well.

We’ve explained this limitation to players over the last year or so, suggesting it could cost as much as $100/mo for a tier with 32k context for Premium models. Surprisingly, many of you have told us, “So what? I’d pay more if we could get access!” To that, we answered both with [Shadow Tiers](https://help.aidungeon.com/faq/about-shadow-tiers), and Ultimate.

In short, Ultimate is NOT the right tier for most players. 32k context is absolutely absurd—that’s about 40 pages of text, and only our most dedicated players have adventures of that length. 128k context is even crazier, at 160-200 pages of text.

Powerful AI models are phenomenal. While they are unquestionably better and more powerful than the normal free models, that performance level is only suited for more discerning players who value quality above all else and are willing to pay for the ability to use them.
